# Ship subject lecture sets with deployments

Lecture PDFs and their prepared search indexes ship with the Vercel deployment; the owner adds or replaces them when updating and deploying the app. This keeps the personal reader's existing content workflow simple and excludes runtime PDF upload storage and administration. Reading, search, tutor context, and citation navigation stay within the active subject, preserving searches across its lectures while keeping identically numbered slides from different subjects distinct.

This is future direction for subject support. The current work stays focused on small quality and speed improvements to the existing reader.
